---
title: Tech radar
rings:
  adopt: We use it with confidence, and reach for it where it fits.
  trial: Worth using on a real project that can absorb some risk.
  assess: Worth exploring, to understand how it would change our work.
  caution: Hold off for now, or use it only with care and a reason.
categories:
  techniques: Ways of working, from how we model data and test it to how we let AI agents change our code.
  tools: What we build and write with day to day, from runtimes and parsers to coding agents and local models.
  platforms: Where our software runs and keeps its data, on servers, at the edge and in the browser.
  languages-frameworks: The languages we write in and the frameworks we build interfaces and systems on.
---

Functional programming and AI tooling, placed in four rings across four categories.
