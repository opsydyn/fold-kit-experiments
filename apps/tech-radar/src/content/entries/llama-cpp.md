llama.cpp runs language models on ordinary hardware, from laptops to servers, with quantised models in the GGUF format. Many local AI tools, Ollama among them, have been built on it.

It stays in Assess. Use it directly when you need control over the build, quantisation or server flags; for everyday local use, a tool built on top of it is easier to live with.
