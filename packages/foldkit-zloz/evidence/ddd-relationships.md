## Delivered contract

- Core owns typed tactical/strategic edge meaning, family-specific guards, an
  immutable builtin rule catalogue, logical node-ID resolution and opt-in editor
  policies. No dependency or runtime adapter is added.
- Tactical references/contains/emits/persists/uses are explicit Zloz conventions.
  Strategic partnership/shared-kernel are symmetric; upstream-downstream and
  customer-supplier are directional, with upstream source and downstream target.
  Side-role annotations are distinct from the relationship pattern.
- Configurable complete catalogues support scoped named custom meanings without
  erasing unknown stored descriptors. Built-in direction and scope are protected.
  Logical relations can inspect group boundaries; ordinary edges still need real
  output/input ports. No fake group ports or second persisted graph is introduced.
- Meaning edits reuse the editor transaction, policy, read-only/gesture and Undo
  boundaries. Role lists are detached and descriptor value equality avoids an
  accidental new Undo entry. History compares changed meaning independently of
  presentation; v1 canonical graph/model/edition envelopes remain unchanged.
- EventStorming resolution/audits ignore other-family descriptors. Causal controls
  show DDD meaning as protected, with no clear/inference path. Causal convention
  readers in the package adapter and Effect CLI require their own family guard.
- Mermaid reports omitted DDD meaning and rejects losses by default. Review packets
  preserve the full snapshot descriptors but do not claim to perform a DDD audit.
